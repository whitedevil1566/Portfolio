THE BRIEF

Turn a menu into a quick decision.

The experience is organized around menu clarity, price visibility and a direct ordering route. The interface keeps the primary choice visible and reduces unnecessary steps between discovery and contact.
WHAT I BUILT

Menu hierarchy + responsive presentation + ordering path.

Menu structureProducts are grouped so visitors can scan the offering without searching through long paragraphs.
Price visibilityItem pricing is exposed close to the menu information instead of being hidden behind another screen.
WhatsApp orderingThe site uses a direct WhatsApp action as the practical conversion path.
Responsive webThe layout is designed around mobile browsing while remaining usable on larger screens.
PROJECT PRINCIPLE

Reduce friction between appetite and action.

For a restaurant site, visual polish matters, but speed of decision matters more. The layout is intentionally direct: show the food, expose the price and make the order path obvious.
