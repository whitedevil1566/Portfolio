THE BRIEF

Make a premium sports platform feel clear.

The product needed to communicate the league quickly: what it is, who participates, where it happens, how to register and where to find supporting content. The public site now gives users direct routes to teams, standings, venues, partners, gallery, FAQ and schedule information.

REAL-WORLD PROOF

Built for an active organization.

The live site currently identifies Toruk Makto as Pakistan's 1st Golf League and lists 150 players, 6 teams, 3 venues and 2 tournaments. Its footer credits the site as “Designed & Built by Abdullah Afsar.”
