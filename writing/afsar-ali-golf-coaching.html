BUILD NOTE · LIVE PROJECT

Designing a coaching website around one clear next step.

Afsar Ali Golf Coaching needed a digital presence that could explain the service quickly, build trust and make booking feel natural.

1. Start with the service

The live site leads with private golf coaching in Karachi and immediately clarifies who the lessons are for: beginners, juniors and experienced golfers.

2. Reduce decision friction

Programs are separated into beginner, junior, private and swing-analysis paths, so visitors can find a relevant offer without scanning one long services list.

3. Build trust before the CTA

The experience uses coaching philosophy, professional background, improvement methods, testimonials and FAQs to answer the questions a new student is likely to have.
